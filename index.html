FAQs

Quick answers

Anything else? Call us on +44 7308 779469.

Is it really free?

Yes. Entry, dinner and the scholarship draw are all free. You will never be asked to pay.

How many slots are there?

Each venue has limited slots. Every registration is reviewed, and registration for a city may close early if it fills up.

When does registration close?

At 23:59 the day before each evening: Wednesday 28 October for London, Sunday 1 November for Birmingham and Wednesday 4 November for Manchester.

What does the scholarship cover?

The full tuition fees, with no upper limit, for one Master's (postgraduate taught) course at a Study Icon partner university in the UK. It does not cover living costs, accommodation, visa fees, the Immigration Health Surcharge or travel, and the winner must meet the university's entry requirements.

Do I have to be there to win?

Yes. Only guests checked in with original photo ID on the night are entered into that evening's draw.

What ID should I bring?

Your original passport or UK photo driving licence. Copies and phone photos are not accepted, and the name must match your registration.

Can I bring a friend?

Each ticket is for one student. Your friend can register for their own slot on this page.

Terms

Scholarship draw terms

The key points: free to enter; one 100% tuition fee scholarship per event; register online by 23:59 the day before your event; attend in person with original photo ID; winner drawn at random on the night.

Read the full terms and conditions
  1. Promoter. Study Icon.
  2. Free entry. The draw is free to enter. No purchase or payment is needed to enter or to win.
  3. Who can enter. UK residents aged 18 or over who hold valid UK immigration permission or settled status. Employees of Study Icon, its group companies and their immediate families cannot enter.
  4. How to enter. (a) Send a seat request through this page by 23:59 (UK time) on the day before your chosen event; (b) receive an approved ticket from Study Icon; (c) attend the event in person and check in with an original passport or UK photo driving licence matching the name on your request. One entry per person, for one event only.
  5. Events and closing dates. London, Thursday 29 October 2026 (requests close 23:59 on 28 October). Birmingham, Monday 2 November 2026 (requests close 23:59 on 1 November). Manchester, Thursday 5 November 2026 (requests close 23:59 on 4 November). Doors open at 17:00 and dinner starts at 18:00.
  6. Seats. Seats at each venue are limited and are given at the Promoter's discretion after reviewing each request. Not every request will receive a ticket.
  7. The prize. One prize per event (three in total): a scholarship covering 100% of the tuition fees, with no upper limit, for one postgraduate taught (Master's) course at a UK partner university of Study Icon. The scholarship is valid only for one-year (full-time) Master's courses at BPP University and cannot be applied to any other university or course length.
  8. What the prize does not cover. Living costs, accommodation, visa application fees, the Immigration Health Surcharge, travel, deposits or any other cost. No cash alternative is offered. The prize cannot be transferred.
  9. Conditions of the prize. The winner must meet the university's academic and English language entry requirements, receive and accept an offer, and meet any UK immigration requirements for the course.
  10. The draw. One winner per event is drawn at random, live at the event, from guests checked in with valid ID. The winner must be present when drawn; if not, another winner is drawn.
  11. Winner announcement. The winner is announced on the night. With the winner's consent, their first name and city may be published on Study Icon's website and social media.
  12. Your data. Personal details are used to manage the event and the draw under the Study Icon privacy policy. Marketing messages are sent only if you opt in.
